Overview

Olympiad Information

About The Olympiad

International Olympiad Coding and Maths 2026 is presented as a global academic competition focused on analytical thinking, algorithmic logic, and mathematical excellence.

India Participant

India is represented by Prashant Gupta, listed with Microsoft in the featured participant roster.

International Platform

Participants from 80 countries are presented on the roster, making the event a broad global stage for young problem-solvers.

Country Representation

Each participant represents their country, combining individual talent with national pride on an international academic stage.

Competition Goal

Participants aim to solve demanding coding and mathematics challenges while competing for top international ranks and recognition.

Competition Areas

Two Challenging Disciplines

IOCM 2026 focuses on the combination of mathematical reasoning and coding ability with intelligence and IQ-based challenges. Participants are expected to show problem-solving skill, dedication, accuracy, speed, and strong preparation.

Winner Benefits

Best Award And Package Growth

The IOCM 2026 winner will be celebrated for exceptional performance in coding and mathematics, with recognition from their country and reward support from their listed company.

01

President's Best Award

The winner will receive the best award from the President of their country as a national honour for outstanding international achievement.

02

Company Increment

The winner's company will give a strong increment in their package to recognise their discipline, talent, and global result.

03

Special Bonus

The company will also provide a best-performance bonus package for winning the international olympiad.
